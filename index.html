The proposal, in one breath.

Divide the country into districts the size of an actual community — about 7,600 people — and the House comes to roughly 43,500 members. At that scale, reputation outcompetes money, neighbors outcompete networks, and Congress becomes something a community can send one of its own into. Not bigness for its own sake. The relationship.
